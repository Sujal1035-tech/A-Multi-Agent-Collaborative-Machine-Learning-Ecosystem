## ✅ Key Recommendations
1.  **Deploy Tuned LightGBM Model**: The `lightgbm_tuned` model achieved the highest cross-validation R2 score (0.855), indicating the best generalization performance among the evaluated models. This model is recommended for making MPG predictions.
2.  **Address Missing Horsepower**: Impute the 6 missing 'horsepower' values. Techniques like mean/median imputation or more sophisticated methods (e.g., KNN imputation) could be considered, depending on the desired accuracy and complexity.
3.  **Investigate Outliers**: Further analyze the identified outliers in 'horsepower' and 'acceleration'. Understand if they represent data entry errors or genuine extreme values. If they are errors, correct them; if genuine, consider their impact and whether models are robust to them or if transformations are needed.
4.  **Feature Engineering**: Explore creating new features by combining or transforming existing ones. For example, ratios like `horsepower/weight` or polynomial features could capture non-linear relationships more effectively.
5.  **Analyze Origin Impact**: Investigate if there are significant differences in MPG based on the 'origin' of the car. This could lead to separate models or feature-specific insights.

## ⚠️ Potential Issues & Warnings
- **Missing Horsepower**: The 1.51% missing values in 'horsepower' need to be addressed before deploying the model for predictions.
- **Outliers in Horsepower and Acceleration**: While not severely impacting all models, the outliers in 'horsepower' (up to 230.0) and 'acceleration' (up to 24.8) might disproportionately influence certain algorithms or lead to less reliable predictions for extreme cases.
- **High Cardinality in 'name'**: The 'name' feature has 305 unique values, making it unsuitable for direct use in most models without significant feature engineering (e.g., one-hot encoding would create too many dimensions, or grouping similar names might be necessary).
- **Overfitting in Decision Tree**: The Decision Tree model exhibits extreme overfitting (1.0 train R2, 0.76 CV R2), highlighting the need for regularization or ensemble methods.
